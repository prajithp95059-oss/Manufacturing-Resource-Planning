The operating loop

Four modules. One continuous loop.

Stackly MRP is built around the actual sequence work moves through on your floor — each module hands its state to the next in real time, so nothing gets re-keyed or re-checked.

01Plan

Demand & capacity planning

Roll forecasts, sales orders and safety stock into a single master schedule that respects real machine and labor capacity — not theoretical throughput.

MRP explosion runs against your live BOM and routing data, flags capacity conflicts before they hit the floor, and re-plans automatically when a rush order lands.

Master SchedulingCapacity PlanningBOM Explosion
02Procure

Purchasing & supplier management

Auto-generate POs from net requirements, track supplier lead-time reliability, and get warned the moment a shortage threatens a scheduled run.

Supplier scorecards update on every receipt, so sourcing decisions are based on how vendors actually perform — not on last year's spreadsheet.

Auto-POVendor ScorecardsShortage Alerts
03Produce

Shop-floor execution

Dispatch work orders to the right cell, capture labor and machine time at the terminal, and watch OEE update live as parts move.

Operators clock in against a job in two taps; supervisors see queue depth, downtime reason codes and scrap in one live view.

Work Order DispatchOEE TrackingQuality Holds
04Deliver

Fulfillment & traceability

Pick, pack and ship against a plan that already knows what's ready — with full lot and serial traceability back to raw material.

Every shipment carries a genealogy record, so a recall or an audit is a query, not a week of archaeology through paper travelers.

Live scheduling

A schedule that moves when the floor does.

Most MRP schedules are stale the moment they're printed. Stackly re-optimizes the sequence continuously against actual machine status, operator availability and material readiness.

  • Drag-and-drop Gantt with automatic constraint checks
  • Finite-capacity scheduling by machine, cell and labor skill
  • Auto-replan on downtime, expedite or material shortfall
Full traceability

Know exactly where every lot has been.

Genealogy is captured automatically as material moves — from incoming inspection through every operation to the shipped serial number.

  • Forward & backward genealogy in one click
  • Non-conformance holds propagate to affected lots instantly
  • Audit-ready export for IATF, AS9100 and FDA reviews
