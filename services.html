Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does implementation take?

+

Most single-plant Growth customers go live in five to eight weeks, including BOM and routing migration, integration and operator training.

Does Stackly integrate with our existing ERP?

+

Yes. Stackly ships with documented REST APIs and pre-built connectors for the major ERP and accounting platforms, plus EDI for supplier and customer transactions.

Can we run Starter and upgrade later without re-implementation?

+

Every tier runs on the same data model, so upgrading unlocks modules without a data migration or a second onboarding.

Is training included?

+

Growth and Enterprise include operator and administrator training as part of onboarding; Starter includes self-serve guides and email support.
